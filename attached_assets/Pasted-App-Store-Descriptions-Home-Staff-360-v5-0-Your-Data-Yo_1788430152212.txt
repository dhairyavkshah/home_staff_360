Full Description (4000 characters max)
Home Staff 360 - Your Data, Your Device, Your Control
The privacy-first staff management platform that keeps YOUR business data on YOUR device. Perfect for households, hotels, restaurants, and service businesses.
PRIVACY-FIRST DESIGN
Unlike other apps, Home Staff 360 stores all your business data locally on your device. We never see your staff records, payments, or expenses. Your sensitive information never touches our servers.
What stays on YOUR device:
- Staff records and profiles
- Attendance logs
- Payment and salary records
- Expenses and receipts
- Invoices and documents
- Client information
- Notes and settings
What we store (minimal):
- Phone number for login
- Chat messages between connected users
- Connection requests
WHY THIS MATTERS
- Your data is truly private - we cannot access it
- No data mining or selling to advertisers
- Complete ownership and control
- GDPR and DPDP Act compliant by design
DUAL USER MODES
Home User Mode (For Employers/Managers):
- Track staff attendance with one tap
- Record payments and advances
- Monitor expenses with approvals
- Generate comprehensive reports
- Connect with your staff in real-time
Staff User Mode (For Service Professionals):
- Manage multiple clients efficiently
- Log attendance at each location
- Track earnings and payment history
- Create professional invoices
- Record expenses with receipt capture
REAL-TIME COLLABORATION
- Connect instantly with staff or clients
- Direct messaging with edit/delete options
- Live attendance updates
- Push notifications for messages
- Auto-connection when adding people
INDUSTRY APPLICATIONS
Households: Manage domestic staff, track laundry services, organize expenses
Hospitality: Coordinate housekeeping teams, track attendance across departments
Restaurants: Monitor kitchen and service staff, manage shift-based hours
Service Businesses: Track field staff, create invoices, manage client locations
Property Management: Coordinate building staff, security personnel
KEY FEATURES
Attendance Tracking
- Mark full day, half day, or leave
- Calendar views and statistics
- Weekly off days and holidays
Payment Management
- Record salaries and advances
- Track pending balances
- Complete transaction history
Expense Tracking
- Categorize by type
- Attach receipt photos
- Monthly and yearly summaries
Invoice Generation
- Create professional invoices
- Share via any app
- Track paid/pending status
SECURITY
- Phone + password authentication
- SMS verification (OTP)
- Optional biometric lock and PIN
- Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
- Secure password hashing
GLOBAL ACCESSIBILITY
Language: English
Currencies: 120+ supported worldwide
Currency Detection: Automatic based on location
FREE WITH ADS OR GO PREMIUM
Free users see brief ads to support development:
- First ad: 30 seconds (supports our team)
- Second ad: Skippable after 5 seconds
Premium subscribers enjoy:
- Completely ad-free experience
- Priority support
- Future premium features
