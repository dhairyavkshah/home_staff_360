# Home Staff 360 Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 3, 2026**

Home Staff 360 is a local-first household and staff record-keeping
application. This policy explains what information the app handles and how you
control it.

## 1. Information stored by the app

When you use Home Staff 360, you may choose to create and store information
such as:

- profile, staff, client, household, and contact records;
- attendance, leave, wage, salary, payment, advance, and expense records;
- laundry records, documents, notes, reminders, and settings; and
- photos, receipt images, attachments, backups, and exported files.

This information is stored in the app's local storage on your device. It is
used only to provide the features you select, such as calculating totals,
displaying history, creating reports, setting reminders, and restoring a local
backup.

Home Staff 360 does not upload this information to a Home Staff 360 server.
The publisher cannot view or retrieve records that remain only in the app's
local storage.

## 2. No account or online service is required

The app does not require or provide:

- an online account, phone-number sign-in, or OTP verification;
- server synchronization or remote collaboration;
- chat, SMS invitations, or remote notifications;
- advertising, advertising profiles, or in-app subscriptions; or
- analytics or tracking of your use of the app.

The app is designed to support everyday record keeping without an internet
connection. If you choose to share an exported file using another app, that
sharing app and its provider may process the file under their own privacy
policies.

## 3. Device permissions and features

The app requests device access only when it is needed for a feature you choose.
Depending on your device and actions, this may include:

- **Camera and photos:** to capture or select profile photos, receipt images,
  document images, or other attachments;
- **Files and storage:** to create, import, restore, or delete backups and
  exported files;
- **Notifications:** to schedule reminders locally on your device; and
- **Device region settings:** to suggest a country or currency using the
  device's locale settings. The app does not collect GPS location for this
  purpose.

You can deny optional permissions. Some features may not work unless the
corresponding permission is granted. Local reminders are generated by the
device; they are not remote push notifications from Home Staff 360.

## 4. Backups, exports, and sharing

Home Staff 360 provides local backup, restore, and export tools. Files created
by these tools are controlled by you. They may contain the records and photos
you choose to save.

On Android, the operating system may back up or transfer app data according to
your device, Google account, and Android backup settings. This is an
operating-system service, not a Home Staff 360 server or synchronization
account. Review your device backup settings if you do not want Android to back
up or transfer app data.

If you send a backup or export through email, messaging, cloud storage, or
another sharing app, the receiving service may store or process that file.
Protect backup files because anyone who obtains a copy may be able to read the
information in it.

## 5. Retention and deletion

Local records remain on the device until you edit or delete them, use the
app's **Clear all local data** action, clear the app's storage, or uninstall
the app. Uninstalling or clearing storage may permanently remove records that
have not been backed up.

Backup and export files are separate copies. Deleting records in the app does
not automatically delete copies that you saved elsewhere. You must delete
those copies from the location where you stored or shared them.

Because the app does not maintain an online account or server copy of your
records, there is no Home Staff 360 account or server database to request
deletion from.

## 6. Security

The app relies on the security controls provided by your device and operating
system, including the app storage sandbox and device lock. You are responsible
for using a secure device lock and for protecting exported and backup files.
No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 7. Information we do not collect

Home Staff 360 does not collect or access your records, photos, contacts, or
files for the publisher's own use. It does not track activity outside the app,
sell personal information, or share information with advertisers.

## 8. Children's privacy

Home Staff 360 is a general-purpose record-keeping tool and is not directed to
children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from
children through an online service because the app does not provide an online
account or server-based collection service.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's privacy practices or features change.
The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when a new version
is published. You should review the policy after an update.

## 10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the Home Staff 360 publisher using the contact
details provided on the app's current store listing.
