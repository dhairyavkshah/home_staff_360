# Home Staff 360 Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 3, 2026**

Home Staff 360 is a local-first, offline application.

## Data storage

Profiles, people, attendance, wages, expenses, notes, photos, settings, and
other records are stored on the user's device. Home Staff 360 does not upload
these records to an application server.

## Accounts and online services

The app does not require an online account, phone sign-in, or OTP verification.
It does not provide remote collaboration, chat, server sync, SMS invitations,
subscriptions, or advertising.

## Device features

The app may request optional device permissions for user-initiated features,
including selecting or taking a profile photo, creating local backup files,
sharing exported files, and scheduling local reminders.

## Backups and deletion

Users control any backup files they create. The **Clear all local data** action
in Profile removes the profile, settings, and app records from the device.
Uninstalling the app may also remove locally stored records.
