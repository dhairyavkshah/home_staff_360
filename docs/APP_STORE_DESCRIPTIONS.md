## Full description

**Home Staff 360 — simple staff management that stays on your device.**

Home Staff 360 is a private, offline organizer for households, home managers,
domestic staff, and service professionals. Keep the everyday details of your
work in one place without creating an online account or sending your records to
a server.

### YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU

Home Staff 360 is designed for local use:

- Your profiles, people, attendance, wages, expenses, photos, documents, and
  notes stay on your device.
- No phone number, online account, or OTP verification is required.
- No internet connection is needed for everyday record keeping.
- No server sync, chat, remote collaboration, advertising, or subscription is
  part of the app.

### HOME MODE

Organize your household or home-service responsibilities:

- Add and manage staff or household people.
- Record daily attendance, half days, leave, weekly offs, and holidays.
- Keep wage, salary, payment, and advance records.
- Track household expenses and attach receipt photos.
- Organize laundry, documents, notes, and other local records.
- Review information in clear, simple screens.

### STAFF MODE

Keep client-home work organized from one local workspace:

- Add and manage client or household records on your device.
- Record attendance for each client or location.
- Track earnings, payments, and pending balances.
- Save expense details and receipt photos.
- Keep notes and supporting documents together.

### BUILT FOR EVERYDAY USE

- Quick attendance updates with calendar views.
- Clear history for wages, payments, and expenses.
- Profile editing with optional photos.
- Local reminders for important tasks.
- Home and Staff modes for different working styles.
- Local backup, restore, and export tools so you control your records.
- Share exported files through the apps available on your device.

Home Staff 360 is a record-keeping tool for household and service workflows.
It does not process payments or require an online connection. Your records are
available on the device where you create them, and any backup or export file is
managed by you.

Bring your staff and household records together with a private app that works
offline and keeps control in your hands.
