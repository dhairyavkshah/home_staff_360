# Home Staff 360 App Store Description

Home Staff 360 helps households and service professionals organize people,
attendance, wages, expenses, laundry, documents, and notes in one private app.

## Private and offline

Your operational records stay on your device. No phone sign-in, OTP, online
account, remote collaboration, subscription, or advertising is required.

## Key features

- Household staff and client-home records
- Attendance, salary, wage, and expense tracking
- Laundry, documents, receipts, and notes
- Local profile and preferences
- User-controlled local backup, restore, and export
